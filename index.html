🚫 No more button-mashing

Every move has its own cooldown. Use the same move 3 times in a row and it locks briefly and hits weaker. Variety is literally stronger here.

🔋 Watch your stamina

The orange bar is stamina. Attacks and dashes spend it, standing and blocking refill it. Run it dry and you'll be tired for a second — easy target!

🛡️ Blocking has a limit too

Block 4 hits quickly and your guard breaks — you get stunned. Sometimes dodging or dashing away is smarter than holding block.

⭐ One ultimate per round

Your ultimate is a round-changer, so you only get one per round. Save it for the moment that actually wins the round.
